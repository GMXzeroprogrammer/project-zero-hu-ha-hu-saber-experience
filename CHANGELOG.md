# Changelog / Version History

[Português (Brasil)](CHANGELOG.pt-BR.md) | [Back to README](README.md)

## v1.7b
- Zero's three-hit ground saber combo is now performed **in place**, like in X4-X6. Hits two and three no longer move Zero forward.

## v1.7
- Boss Rush is now a dedicated main-menu option: **New Game / Load Game / Options / Boss Rush**.
- Unlocks after at least one save has completed the game; it does not use or change that save.
- Starts with all weapons, armor/upgrades, hearts, chips, and four full sub-tanks. Select on the title screen can choose Zero.
- Boss order is randomized. After each victory, restore 10-30% HP; overflow goes to sub-tanks.
- Completing, leaving, or getting a Game Over in Boss Rush returns to the title screen.
- Removed the former X-button shortcut on the Load Game screen.

## v1.6
- Rebalanced normal enemy saber damage based on X4: first slash = one normal shot; second slash = one normal shot; third slash = two normal shots.
- Aerial slash deals about two normal shots of damage; spinning slash deals a smaller amount per rotation.
- Against bosses: first and second slashes deal one damage each; the third deals two.
- Fixed the first slash occasionally hitting an enemy twice after the next slash was buffered.

## v1.5
- Fixed Zero's Z-Buster projectile registering repeated hits on a single enemy. Each projectile now hits only once.
- Fixed saber attacks being overly powerful against regular enemies by ensuring each attack hits an enemy at most once and reducing its damage.
- Purple boss health bar: 50% extra in normal play, 100% after clearing the game.

## v1.4
- Added a visible, **purple extra boss-health bar** layered over the original. The extra portion drains before the standard yellow bar.
- Introduced Boss Rush via the Load Game screen (superseded by the dedicated menu option in v1.7).
- Random boss order, full sub-tanks at start, partial HP recovery after wins, no save modification.
- Current roster: eight Mavericks and two Doppler fortress bosses. Doppler, Sigma, Vile, Bit, Byte, and other fortress bosses remain to be implemented.

## v1.3
- Fixed pink charge particles for Zero's Buster.
- Falling into pits no longer triggers character swapping; immediate death behaves as in the original game.
- Split saber damage among the three combo hits and limited bosses to one hit per swing.
- Added the spinning aerial saber attack (Down + Y while airborne).
- Introduced extra boss health, later visualized by the purple health bar.

## v1.2
- Fixed stray green graphic tiles appearing during the saber animations.

## v1.1
- Added X4-inspired Zero saber sprites, a three-hit ground combo, and an aerial slash.
- Reworked hitboxes for the new animation frames.
- Limited the Zero Buster charge to the pink level and removed the green-charge saber finish.
- Fixed a single Y press occasionally causing two attacks.
